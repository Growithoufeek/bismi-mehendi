How to Order Aari Embroidery

From your first design reference to the completed garment, here is the information to discuss with BISMI before confirming a custom Aari order.

Start an Aari EnquiryView Designs

A simple path from idea to embroidery

Every custom garment is different. The first conversation gives BISMI the information needed to understand the design, garment and occasion before discussing availability and a quotation.

The six enquiry steps

1. Share your requirement

Send a design reference, inspiration image or a description of the Aari work you want.

2. Discuss the design

Talk through the garment, preferred coverage, motifs, colours and other details you want to consider.

3. Receive a quotation

BISMI reviews the design and work required before sharing an exact quotation.

4. Confirm the order

Once the requirements and quotation are agreed, the order can be discussed for scheduling.

5. Handcrafted work

The embroidery is completed according to the agreed requirements for the garment.

6. Completion

Collection or delivery arrangements should be confirmed directly for the individual order.

What should I send when enquiring?

  • Reference image or design direction
  • Blouse, saree, lehenga or other garment type
  • Desired design or embroidery coverage
  • Wedding or event date
  • Any special requirement you already know

Important order details

Payment, deposit, garment submission, measurements, courier, delivery, returns and urgent-order policies require owner confirmation. Do not assume these arrangements before speaking with BISMI.

Before you send your message

Do I need to know the final design?

No. You can start with an inspiration image or explain the style you are considering. BISMI can discuss what is required for the specific garment.

Will the quotation be the same for every blouse?

No. The quotation depends on the design and work required, including factors such as coverage, customization and handwork.

How do I check availability?

Send your event date and requirement to BISMI through WhatsApp. Current availability should be confirmed directly.
