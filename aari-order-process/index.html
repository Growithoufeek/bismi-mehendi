How It Works

How Aari Ordering Works at BISMI

From your first message to a finished blouse — here's exactly what happens, step by step.

Start on WhatsApp

Send a reference

Share a photo of the design you like — bridal, custom, floral or your own idea — over WhatsApp, along with your blouse fabric colour.

Discuss the design

We talk through what's achievable on your fabric, the coverage you want, and any changes to the reference.

Get a quotation

Based on the design's complexity, coverage and materials, we give you a clear price and timeline.

Confirm your order

Once you're happy with the design and price, we confirm your slot — we only take a limited number of orders each month.

Embroidery begins

Your piece is hand-worked on the Aari frame. Heavy bridal work typically takes 30–50 hours; lighter custom work less.

Completion & handover

We share progress photos where useful, and once finished, arrange pickup or courier delivery.

Ready to start?

Message us your reference design on WhatsApp and we'll take it from there.
