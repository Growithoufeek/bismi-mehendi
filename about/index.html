About BISMI

A handcrafted Aari embroidery business from Pandaravadai, Thanjavur, with Mehendi cones as a secondary offering.

Crafted patiently, one piece at a time

BISMI’s existing story describes a homemaker from Pandaravadai dedicated to preserving Aari embroidery. Blouses, sarees and lehengas are handcrafted for weddings, bridesmaids, festive occasions and custom requirements.

The existing website states that individual pieces may take approximately 30–50 hours of handwork and that only a limited number of orders are accepted each month. Exact expectations should be discussed for each design.

Aari is the main focus

The website is built around high-value custom embroidery enquiries. The primary path is to view the portfolio, understand the pricing factors, then send a reference through WhatsApp.
