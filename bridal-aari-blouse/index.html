Heavy & Bridal Embroidery

Bridal Aari Blouse Work — Thanjavur

Full, heavy Aari embroidery for the wedding blouse — beads, zari, mirror and thread work built up over 30–50 hours by hand. We take a limited number of bridal orders each month, so the earlier you reach out, the more design freedom you'll have.

Designs we work with

Bridal Aari blouses at BISMI are built around your blouse cut, blouse colour and how much of the surface you want covered — from a heavy full-front to a lighter back-and-sleeve focus. Common directions our brides ask for:

Full bridal coverage

Dense beadwork and zari across the entire front and back — the heaviest, most time-intensive option.

Floral & traditional motifs

Classic South Indian floral and temple-inspired patterns, worked in Aari chain stitch with mirror or stone accents.

Sleeve & back focus

A lighter front with the heavy detailing concentrated on the back or sleeves — popular for reception blouses.

Heavy vs light bridal work

"Heavy" and "light" aren't just about looks — they change the timeline and the price.

Heavy bridal workLighter bridal work
Dense, edge-to-edge coverage with beads, zari and mirrorsFocused motifs with visible fabric in between
Typically 35–50+ hours of handworkTypically 15–30 hours of handwork
Best for the main wedding blouseWorks well for reception, engagement or family-function blouses

Not sure which suits your blouse? Send us your fabric colour and the occasion, and we'll suggest what fits.

Customization

Every bridal order is customized to your blouse fabric, your colour palette and the reference design you send — we don't run fixed, ready-made bridal designs. If you have a specific reference photo (even from someone else's blouse), send it and we'll tell you what's achievable on your fabric.

Bridal FAQs

How early should I book bridal Aari work?
Because we take limited monthly orders and heavy bridal pieces need 30–50 hours, we recommend reaching out at least 6–8 weeks before your wedding date — earlier if your date falls in peak wedding season.
Can I get a design similar to a photo I found online?
Yes, send the reference on WhatsApp and we'll tell you what's achievable in Aari work on your blouse fabric.
Do you make the reception blouse too?
Yes — many brides order a lighter Aari blouse for reception or engagement alongside the main bridal piece.
