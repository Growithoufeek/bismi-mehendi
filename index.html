Our Story

I’m a homemaker from Pandaravadai, Thanjavur, dedicated to preserving the art of Aari embroidery. Every blouse, saree, and lehenga is handcrafted with patience and love — taking 30–50 hours per piece. I take only a handful of orders each month. Alongside, I offer handmade henna cones as a small extension of my craft. At BISMI, tradition isn’t just preserved—it’s celebrated 💫

Why Mehendi Cones?

Why Aari Embroidery?

Aari embroidery transforms any blouse or saree into a one-of-a-kind statement piece. Each stitch is crafted by hand over many hours, ensuring elegance that lasts and stands out. I accept only a limited number of orders each month to maintain the highest standard of craftsmanship.
