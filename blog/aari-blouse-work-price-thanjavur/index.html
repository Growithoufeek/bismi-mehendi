What Affects Aari Blouse Work Pricing?

A custom Aari blouse quotation depends on the design and work required, not on a single universal price.

Common pricing factors

  • Design complexity
  • Embroidery coverage
  • Garment type
  • Customization
  • Materials involved, when confirmed
  • Amount of handwork
  • Turnaround requirements and availability

How to receive an exact quotation

Send the reference image, garment type and event date through WhatsApp. BISMI can discuss the requirement and provide an exact quotation.
