What Is Aari Embroidery?

A simple introduction to the handwork behind custom Aari blouses, saree borders and festive garments.

A craft discussed around the garment

Aari embroidery is a form of hand embroidery used to build decorative work on garments. The final discussion depends on the garment, reference design, desired coverage and occasion.

What should you decide first?

  • Which garment will receive the work?
  • Do you have a reference image?
  • What event or occasion is it for?
  • What level of coverage are you considering?
