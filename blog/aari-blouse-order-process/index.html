How the Aari Blouse Order Process Works

A clear first message helps BISMI understand your garment and discuss the right next step.

What to send in your first message

  1. Reference image or design direction
  2. Garment type
  3. Wedding or event date
  4. Preferred coverage or details
  5. Your location

What happens next?

BISMI can discuss the design, current availability, requirements and quotation through WhatsApp. Booking, payment, garment submission and delivery arrangements should be confirmed directly for the order.
