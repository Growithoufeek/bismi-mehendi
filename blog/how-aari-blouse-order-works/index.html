How to Order a Custom Aari Blouse

A well-prepared first message helps BISMI understand your garment, design direction, event date and quotation requirement.

See the Full Order ProcessStart an Enquiry

From inspiration to finished work

A custom blouse order begins with a conversation. You do not need to have every detail decided, but a useful reference and event date make it easier to discuss the work.

The customer journey

1. Find inspiration

Browse real work and save references that show the design direction you like.

2. Send the reference

Share the image with your garment type, event date and preferred coverage.

3. Discuss the design

Talk through customization, motifs, colours and the requirements of your blouse.

4. Get a quotation

The quote is based on the design and work required. There is no universal price.

5. Confirm

Confirm the agreed requirements and current availability directly with BISMI.

6. Embroidery and completion

The work is completed for the garment, with final collection or delivery arrangements confirmed for the order.

Message checklist

  • Reference image
  • Blouse or garment type
  • Wedding or event date
  • Preferred design or coverage
  • Special requirements
