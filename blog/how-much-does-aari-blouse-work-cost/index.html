How Much Does Aari Blouse Work Cost?

There is no universal price for a custom Aari blouse. The design and work required need to be understood before a quotation can be prepared.

See Pricing FactorsSend Your Design

Why quotations vary

Two blouse designs can appear similar while requiring different amounts of embroidery, coverage, customization and handwork. A useful quotation should be based on the actual reference and garment rather than a generic number.

Factors that influence cost

Design complexity

Detailed patterns can require a different amount of work from a simpler design.

Coverage

The area and placement of embroidery on the garment affect the work required.

Materials

Materials and embellishments should be discussed and confirmed for the specific order.

Customization

Changes to a reference design may affect the work needed.

Handwork

Individual pieces involve handwork, and the existing website notes approximately 30–50 hours for a piece.

Garment and timing

Garment type and required completion time can be part of the quotation discussion.

What to send for an exact quote

  1. Reference image
  2. Blouse or garment type
  3. Preferred design or coverage
  4. Wedding or event date
  5. Any special requirements
