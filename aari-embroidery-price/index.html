Why Aari work is quoted individually

A custom quotation depends on the garment and the work discussed. Two blouse designs may need different amounts of handwork, coverage and customization.

Factors that can affect the quotation

  • Design complexity
  • Embroidery coverage
  • Garment type
  • Customization and reference requirements
  • Materials involved, where confirmed for the order
  • Amount of handwork required
  • Turnaround requirements, subject to availability

How to ask for pricing

Share your design reference, garment type and event date on WhatsApp. BISMI can discuss the requirements and provide an exact quotation.
