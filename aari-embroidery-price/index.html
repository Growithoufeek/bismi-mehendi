Pricing

Aari Embroidery Price — What It Actually Depends On

Aari work is priced by the hours and materials that go into your specific piece, so we don't publish a fixed price list. Here's exactly what changes the cost, so you know what to tell us for an accurate quote.

Send Your Design for a Quote

What affects Aari embroidery cost

Design complexity

Intricate motifs with fine detailing take longer to execute than simple, repeating patterns.

Coverage

How much of the blouse or fabric is covered — a full bridal front costs more than a border or sleeve accent.

Materials

Zari thread, beads, stones and mirrors each add to material cost, on top of the handwork.

Amount of handwork

Dense, layered stitching (common in heavy bridal work) simply takes more hours than lighter designs.

Customization

Fully custom designs built around your reference take more planning than a design we've already worked before.

Garment & urgency

The fabric you're working on and how soon you need it finished can also affect the final price.

We don't quote a price without seeing the design. Send a reference photo and your fabric/blouse colour on WhatsApp, and we'll walk you through what it would take and what it would cost.

Bridal vs custom work

As a rough guide: bridal blouses with heavy, full coverage sit at the top of the range because of the 30–50 hours of handwork involved, while lighter custom pieces for festive or family-function wear cost less and take fewer hours. The only way to get an accurate number is to send your specific design.

How to get an accurate quote

  1. Send a clear reference photo on WhatsApp
  2. Tell us the fabric/blouse colour and occasion
  3. Let us know your rough timeline
  4. We reply with what's possible and the price
