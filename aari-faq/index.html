About the craft and service

What is Aari embroidery?

Aari embroidery is a traditional hand-embroidery technique used for detailed surface work on garments. BISMI uses it for custom blouses, sarees, lehengas and special-occasion work.

What types of Aari work does BISMI offer?

The existing business information includes custom blouses, bridal and bridesmaid work, saree borders, saree and lehenga work, and festive or occasion garments. Specific requirements should be confirmed before ordering.

Do you make bridal Aari blouses?

Yes. Bridal Aari blouse enquiries are a primary service. Visit the bridal Aari blouse page to prepare your enquiry.

Can I provide a reference design?

Yes, customers can send a reference image or discuss a preferred design direction. BISMI should confirm whether the specific reference can be taken up.

How much does Aari embroidery cost?

There is no universal price on this website. Cost varies by design, coverage, garment, customization, materials and work required. Use the pricing guide and send your reference for an exact quotation.

How long does Aari embroidery take?

The existing website says an individual piece may take approximately 30–50 hours of handwork. Actual timing depends on the design and current schedule, so it must be confirmed for each order.
