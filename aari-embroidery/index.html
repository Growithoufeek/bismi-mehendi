What Aari embroidery means at BISMI

Aari embroidery is the primary work of BISMI Aari & Mehendi Center. The existing business story describes a homemaker from Pandaravadai preserving the craft through patient handwork on blouses, sarees and lehengas.

Each design conversation begins with the actual garment, your occasion and the reference you want to explore. This keeps the enquiry practical and allows the quotation to reflect the work required.

Types of work to discuss

Custom blouses

Explore a bridal or bridesmaid requirement with your event date and design direction.

Saree borders

Ask about handcrafted saree border work and how a reference can be adapted.

How the enquiry works

  1. Send a design reference or inspiration image.
  2. Tell BISMI the garment type and event date.
  3. Discuss the desired coverage and custom details.
  4. Confirm availability and receive an exact quotation.

Frequently asked questions

Where is BISMI located?

BISMI is based in Pandaravadai, Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu.

Can I send my own design reference?

Yes, a reference can be shared for discussion. BISMI should confirm whether the specific design can be taken up.

How long does Aari work take?

The existing website says individual pieces may take approximately 30–50 hours of handwork. Actual timing depends on the design and should be confirmed for the order.

How is the price decided?

Price varies by design, garment, coverage, customization and work required. See the pricing guide.

Have a design in mind?

Send the reference and tell us about the garment. We will discuss the work required and availability.
