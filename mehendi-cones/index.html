Mehendi Cones — Fresh, Natural, Handmade in Thanjavur

Alongside our Aari embroidery, BISMI prepares natural henna cones in small weekly batches from Pandaravadai, Thanjavur — chemical-free, skin-friendly, and made for a deep, lasting stain. Available as single cones, sets, or bulk for artists and parlours.

Chemical-free, made the traditional way — no harsh additives, safe for regular use on skin.
